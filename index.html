A specialist-led approach

Orthodontics shaped around your needs.

Choosing orthodontic treatment can involve more than deciding which type of brace to have. Your teeth, bite, jaw position, dental history and wider treatment needs all matter.

Joanne Birdsall is a specialist orthodontist with experience in straightforward and complex orthodontic care. Her work includes treatment planning for patients who may need a coordinated approach involving other dental and surgical professionals.

The aim is to help you understand your options clearly, consider the clinical factors involved and make informed decisions about the next stage of your care.

Helpful information

Questions patients often ask.

These answers provide a starting point. The most appropriate treatment and appointment arrangements can only be confirmed for your individual circumstances.

Contact the practice →
Can adults have orthodontic treatment?
Yes. Adults may be suitable for orthodontic treatment. The options depend on dental health, bite, treatment goals and an individual clinical assessment.
What is the difference between fixed braces and Invisalign?
Fixed braces use brackets and wires attached to the teeth. Invisalign uses a planned series of removable clear aligners. Neither option is suitable for every case, so a specialist assessment is important.
Can orthodontic treatment help with complex bite concerns?
Specialist orthodontic treatment can form part of the management of complex bite and jaw concerns. Some cases require a coordinated plan with other dental or surgical specialists.
What happens at the first consultation?
The first consultation involves discussing your concerns and assessing your teeth and jaws. Photographs, radiographs or a 3D scan may be used where appropriate. Some assessments may require more than one appointment.
