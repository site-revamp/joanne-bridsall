Joanne Birdsall Orthodontics — Homepage Redesign Documentation

1. Website Overview

Joanne Birdsall Orthodontics is a specialist clinician-led orthodontic service operating across practice affiliations in Yorkshire, England (including Leeds, Pontefract, Bawtry, and Normanton). Led by specialist orthodontist Joanne Birdsall (GDC 82176), the practice provides comprehensive orthodontic care ranging from aesthetic aligners and conventional braces to complex dentofacial treatments and pre-surgical planning.

Business Purpose & Target Audience

The website serves to inform prospective patients and healthcare partners about available specialist orthodontic treatments, establish clinical trust, and facilitate patient consultation requests. Target audiences include adult patients, teenagers/children needing alignment, patients seeking discreet options (Invisalign, lingual braces), and complex multidisciplinary cases (hypodontia, jaw surgery preparation, implant space planning).

Homepage Redesign Direction

The new homepage design provides a refined, professional digital storefront tailored to a specialist medical practice. It highlights clinical expertise, clearly categorizes treatment pathways, and simplifies how patients access care across multiple practice affiliations in Yorkshire.

2. Main Improvements

Fix 1: Clarified Multi-Location Practice Affiliations & Contact Pathways

Previous Issue: Practice location listings (Leeds, Pontefract, Bawtry, Normanton) and contact information lacked clarity regarding central contact details vs. independent practice affiliations.

What Was Changed: Structured clear practice location cards explicitly distinguishing professional practice affiliations, providing transparent contact routes via the official contact page (/contact/) and verifying location-specific contact channels (e.g., One Smile Dental contact line: 01977 233 139).

Impact: Eliminates geographic confusion for patients across Yorkshire while maintaining accurate administrative representation.

Fix 2: Prominent Specialist Credentialing & Clinical Background Showcase

Previous Issue: Highlighted qualifications, national accolades (J.K. Williams Gold Medal), and hospital consultancy experience were diluted within general text, obscuring clinical authority.

What Was Changed: Created a dedicated clinical background summary section directly on the homepage, explicitly displaying core credentials (BChD, MFDS RCPSG, MSc, IMOrth RCPSG, FDS Orth RCPSG, PG Cert Ed, PG Dip Clin Ed, GDC 82176) and professional achievements.

Impact: Instantly builds patient confidence by establishing high-level clinical authority and specialist credentials upfront.

Fix 3: Categorized Treatment Options & Multidisciplinary Service Mapping

Previous Issue: Diverse treatment options—from aesthetic appliances to complex surgical preparations—were presented without clear categorization based on patient needs.

What Was Changed: Reorganized treatments into clear patient categories (Adults, Teenagers, Discreet Appliances such as Invisalign and Lingual Braces) alongside advanced clinical services (hypodontia management, implant space preparation, and surgical collaboration).

Impact: Enables prospective patients and referring clinicians to rapidly identify relevant treatment paths and specialist capabilities.

3. Design Improvements

Visual Presentation & Layout: Modernized the interface with a clean, healthcare-focused aesthetic, balanced typography, and structured content blocks.

User Experience (UX): Streamlined patient navigation to primary service information (/services/) and about pages (/about/), featuring clear Call-To-Action elements for new patient consultations.

Consistency & Appearance: Standardized visual formatting across treatment lists, clinical credential badges, and multi-location listings for a cohesive presentation.
